## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, mobile-first athletic visual language tailored for the provincial branch of the national domino sports federation in Papua Barat Daya. The identity merges institutional sports governance with the vibrant cultural richness of Southwest Papua—drawing inspiration from Raja Ampat oceanic wave currents, Malamoi geometric spirals, and stylized Cenderawasih feather motifs.

The brand persona is disciplined, prestigious, spirited, and regional:
- **Disciplined & Institutional:** Official federation status communicated through precise typography, high-contrast tournament tables, and structured credentialing badges.
- **Athletic & Strategic:** Mind-sports energy reflecting calculated tactical mastery, live tournament pacing, and clean scoreboard analytics.
- **Culturally Rooted:** Elegant tribal geometry distilled into vector hairline borders, accent headers, and textured card divider lines without crowding screen space.

The primary style is **Modern Athletic Minimalism with Cultural Geometric Accents**. It features crisp surfaces on light slate, controlled deep navy structural anchors, vibrant federation red action accents, and razor-sharp data visualization for match fixtures, brackets, and domino pip scoring.

## Colors

The color palette reflects official Indonesian national domino sports standards augmented by Southwest Papuan oceanic and regional tones:

- **Primary (`#D32F2F` - Federation Crimson):** Represents national competitive drive, official decisions, live match pips, tournament registration triggers, and active status indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0D253F` - Malamoi Deep Navy):** Serves as the structural framework, anchoring top app bars, primary card containers, bottom navigation, and authoritative typography.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Raja Ampat Ocean Azure):** Delivers clean sports tech contrast for brackets, round badges, referee metadata, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Light Slate Canvas):** Provides an athletic, crisp backdrop, preventing eye fatigue during extended tournament monitoring while framing card containers cleanly.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `gold-champion` (`#EAB308`): Dedicated solely to podium ranks, championship trophies, and gold medal standings.
  - `raja-turquoise` (`#06B6D4`): Applied in gradient trims and vector wave ornaments.
  - `navy-midnight` (`#061524`): Used for elevated dark surfaces like credential badges and live broadcast headers.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects precision and modern athleticism:
- **Headlines & Scores (Outfit):** A geometric, high-energy sans-serif optimized for athletic headlines, live match scores, seed numbers, and badge labels. Numbers remain uniform and tabular when displaying game tallies and clock counters.
- **Body Text & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A clean, highly legible humanist sans-serif with open apertures and balanced proportions, ensuring clarity in dense match fixtures, news reports, rulebooks, and participant rosters on small mobile viewports.

Rules:
- Uppercase styling combined with `letterSpacing: 0.04em` or `0.06em` is strictly reserved for `label-md` and `label-sm` when designating tournament phases (e.g., `BABAK PENYISIHAN`, `FINAL POOL A`, `LIVE SCORE`).
- Primary scores within tournament cards must render at `headline-md` or `headline-lg` in `Outfit` with semi-bold or bold weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an adaptive, mobile-first grid built around an 8pt base unit (scaled down to 4pt micro-increments for athletic scorecard metrics):

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px):** Single-column stack with an outer canvas padding of `margin` (`1rem`), utilizing `gutter` (`1rem`) between card rows. Dense scorecard tables contract to `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tile layout cells.
- **Tablet / Expanded Viewports (600px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid system with `1.25rem` outer margins. Match fixtures and news feed display in a 2-column card orientation.
- **Desktop Dashboard (>1024px):** Max container width capped at `1200px` centered, utilizing a 12-column grid with `gutter` of `1.5rem`.

Spacing principles:
- Information-dense zones (e.g., domino pip tables, player versus player head-to-head records) utilize compact vertical padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to maximize visible screen real estate.
- Editorial sections (official announcements, regional news, leadership addresses) utilize generous breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces and sports-grade ambient shadows tinted with deep navy:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background base surface `#F8FAFC` (Canvas).
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` fill with an ultra-thin perimeter border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 37, 63, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Match / Live Card):** Elevated state with a deeper navy-tinted shadow: `0px 8px 20px -4px rgba(13, 37, 63, 0.12)` accompanied by an active left-border accent in `#D32F2F` (3px width).
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Floating Actions):** Surface elevation for filters, quick score entry, and tournament draw modals: `0px 16px 32px -6px rgba(13, 37, 63, 0.20)` with high-grade backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Cultural Texture Overlay:** Subtle, low-opacity (4–6%) geometric Malamoi spiral curves or Raja Ampat wave borders applied as an SVG vector background layer on top navigation headers and credential badges.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius) to evoke athletic agility while retaining clean geometric discipline:

- **Standard Cards & Modals:** Standard corners at `0.75rem` (`12px`) for card wraps, matching the rounded contour of competition domino tiles.
- **Pills & Status Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for match statuses (`LIVE`, `SELESAI`, `TERTUNDA`), category chips (`GANDA PUTRA`, `PERORANGAN`), and floating navigation bars.
- **Domino Tile Indicators:** Miniature score icons and domino bone indicators feature a `4px` corner radius with crisp division lines matching the physical white-and-black bone tile standard.
- **Ornamental Motif Dividers:** Angular 45-degree micro-cuts on badge edges and card header caps inspired by traditional Papuan wood carving geometric borders.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Athletic Button:** Bold `#D32F2F` background with white `Outfit` semi-bold text, `12px` border radius, micro-tactile active push transition (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Federation Button:** Deep Navy `#0D253F` solid or outline button with a 1.5px solid border, paired with crisp white or navy text.
- **Quick Action Bar:** Fixed bottom-screen floating capsule container featuring navigation items and a central crimson `+` trigger for instant scorecard submission.

### Tournament Cards & Fixtures
- **Live Match Card:** White surface with an integrated header bar featuring tournament title and pool info. Left side exhibits Player/Pair A, right side Player/Pair B, with a central high-contrast scoreboard displaying aggregate points and remaining domino count. Live games flash a miniature `#EF4444` indicator pip.
- **Cultural Motif Header:** Dark navy container `#0D253F` featuring faint vector-engraved Malamoi waves and Cenderawasih feather silhouettes along the right boundary, accompanied by gold provincial designation text.

### Domino Tile Displays
- **Tile Component:** Skeuomorphic-flat domino bone proportioned 1:2 with a central hairline divider, recessed circular pips (dots) rendered in deep navy or federation red for double-six sets.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Chips:** Horizontal scroll container with rounded-full chips. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E2E8F0` border and `#475569` text. Active: `#0D253F` surface with `#FFFFFF` text and subtle glow.

### Badges & Official Credentials
- **Federation Verification Badge:** Dual-layered shield icon containing the official ORADO emblem, regional gold banner text "PENGPROV PAPUA BARAT DAYA", and an official QR verification stamp.

### Input Fields & Score Counters
- **Score Input Field:** Numerical keypad-optimized inputs with centered `Outfit` tabular digits, bold focus rings in `#D32F2F`, and rapid `+1` / `-1` stepper buttons for instant tallying at the domino table.
